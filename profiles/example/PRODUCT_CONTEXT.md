# Example SaaS Product Context

This file gives CQ agents a compact product map for the bundled example SaaS company. It is intentionally generic and safe to publish. Replace it with your own profile context when creating a real deployment.

## Company in one page

ExampleCo is a small B2B SaaS company with:

- **Product app** — customer-facing web application, onboarding, account settings, billing, and core workflows.
- **Data and analytics** — product metrics, activation/retention funnels, customer feedback, and reporting.
- **Growth system** — website, SEO, content, lifecycle email, sales collateral, and partner research.
- **Customer operations** — support workflows, help docs, customer interviews, and renewal/expansion feedback.
- **Internal tooling** — automation, project boards, runbooks, QA, and release coordination.

## Strategic model

- **Acquire** — attract the right customers through clear positioning, evidence-backed marketing, and useful demos.
- **Activate** — get new users to a real first success quickly.
- **Retain** — improve reliability, UX, support, and product value so customers keep using the product.
- **Expand** — find honest upgrade paths based on measurable customer value.

## Core product/system areas

### Web application

User onboarding, core workflows, account/team management, settings, notifications, billing surfaces, and in-app education.

### Backend/platform

APIs, background jobs, data storage, authentication, integrations, observability, security basics, and operational runbooks.

### Analytics and research

Activation metrics, churn/retention signals, customer interviews, support themes, funnel analysis, A/B experiments, and competitive research.

### Growth and marketing

Website messaging, landing pages, SEO, product launches, campaigns, email sequences, social content, sales enablement, and partner research.

### Operations

Issue triage, release coordination, quality gates, incident review, documentation hygiene, and team rituals.

## Source-of-truth model

For this sample setup:

- **GitHub Issues** — durable task contracts, review history, and agent output.
- **GitHub Projects** — visible queue and status tracking.
- **Profile docs** — company context, role instructions, and operating conventions.
- **Human approval** — required for external publishing, commercial commitments, and high-risk claims.

## Approval boundary

Agents may draft and analyze, but humans approve before anything is published, sent, priced, promised, or committed externally. This is especially strict for:

- public communications
- sales or partnership outreach
- pricing/discount/commercial terms
- legal, financial, security, compliance, or performance claims
- official roadmap/product/delivery commitments
- statements that imply company authority
